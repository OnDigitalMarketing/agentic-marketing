# 01_acquisition

Read `../SKILL.md` first.

## What this folder is for

The economics of getting a customer. Where they came from, what it cost, how long it took, and whether that repeats at larger volume.

## Data folder

`00_acquisition_data`. First-touch and last-touch source by customer, spend by channel and period, lead-to-customer conversion rates, sales cycle length, first order value, and your own Customer Acquisition Cost working file.

## How work gets done

1. Start with total spend divided by new customers for the period. Blended Customer Acquisition Cost is crude and it is honest, and it is the number most ventures skip straight past.
2. Then split by channel, and say plainly how much of the split is attribution modeling rather than observation.
3. Separate paid from organic and from referral. A blended number that hides free word of mouth will make a paid channel look better than it is.
4. Compare against Customer Lifetime Value from `../02_retention`. Acquisition cost only means something next to what a customer is worth.
5. State the payback period in months. A channel that pays back in four months and one that pays back in twenty are different businesses.

## The trap here

Attribution is a model, not a measurement. Every platform claims the conversions it can see, and the claims sum to more than the total. Report what you observed, then report what you modeled, and never blend the two into one confident number.

## Guardrails

- State sample size. Customer Acquisition Cost computed on eleven customers is a hypothesis.
- Do not report a channel cost per acquisition without saying whether it includes creative and staff time.
- If the period had an unusual event, say so before quoting the number.

## Definition of done

Blended acquisition cost, channel split with its method named, payback period, sample size, and a comparison to customer value.
