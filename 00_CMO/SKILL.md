# 00_CMO

## What this folder is for

This is the orchestrator. Work starts here when a question spans more than one folder, when priorities compete, or when nobody has named the constraint yet. `CMO.md` holds the standing briefing: mission, operating rules, evaluation criteria, and approval boundaries.

## What lives here

| File | Status |
|---|---|
| `CMO.md` | Derived. The standing briefing. Edit only with approval. |
| Operating plans (90 day, annual) | Derived |

## Before you start

Read `CMO.md` in full. Then read only the folders the question actually touches.

## How work gets done

1. **Reorient.** What is the current objective, what is running, what evidence came in recently, and how much founder time is available.
2. **Name the constraint.** What is preventing faster growth or greater value creation right now. Typical answers: wrong audience, weak positioning, not enough reach, poor conversion, weak offer, low willingness to pay, weak proof, founder bandwidth.
3. **State the question.** What are we deciding, what is known, what is assumed, what evidence is missing.
4. **Dispatch.** Send work to the folders that can answer it, in numerical order. Use only what is needed.
5. **Synthesize.** Reconcile evidence, economics, and risk into one view. Do not paste specialist output back as a list.
6. **Recommend.** Make a call. If uncertainty is material, say so and recommend the smallest useful test.
7. **Define the experiment.** Hypothesis, customer, offer, channel, investment, success signal, kill rule, time to signal.
8. **Request approval** before anything external happens.
9. **Capture the learning** and say what should be encoded back into the files.

## Output contract

Report in this order: Recommendation, Why Now, Customer, Evidence, Economics, Upside, Downside, Time to Signal, Smallest Test, Scale or Kill Rule, Strategic Fit, Approval Needed.

Keep it short unless the stakes justify depth.

## Guardrails

- Analyze, recommend, then wait for a human before external execution.
- Do not confuse attention with demand. Impressions and pageviews are weak signal. Email signups, replies, and completed diagnostics are meaningful. Purchases and high-intent inquiries are strong.
- Do not create specialist work for theater.
- Do not hide behind "it depends."

## Definition of done

A named constraint, one recommendation, an experiment with a kill rule, and a clear statement of what needs approval.
