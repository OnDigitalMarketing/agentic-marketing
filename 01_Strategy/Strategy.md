# Strategy

Positioning answers who you are for, what you do that the alternatives do not, and why that matters to the buyer. The plan turns that into a sequence with dates and numbers.

**Fill this in after `02_Customers`, not before.** Positioning written without the customer file in front of you is guesswork with better formatting.

## How work gets done here

1. Read `00_CMO/CMO.md`, then `02_Customers/Customers.md`.
2. Work through the five components below in order. Each asks for evidence, and "I believe" is a valid answer as long as you label it a hypothesis.
3. Mark every claim as evidence, inference, or hypothesis.
4. Log what changed and why at the bottom.

A positioning change is material. It needs approval before it goes anywhere public.

## Guardrails

- Avoid commodity claims: AI-powered, data-driven, innovative, customer-centric, best-in-class, cutting-edge.
- Credentials are proof, not positioning. The customer is the protagonist.
- If the positioning contradicts something in `02_Customers`, the customer data wins and you say so before continuing.
