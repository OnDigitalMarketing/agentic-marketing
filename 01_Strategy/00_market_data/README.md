# 00_market_data

**Source. Read only. Never committed.**

Git ignores every folder ending in `_data`. Only this README is tracked, so real files stay on your machine.

## What goes here

- Competitor pricing pages, positioning copy, and feature comparisons you captured
- Analyst or industry reports you have a license to read
- Category and market sizing research
- Job postings from competitors, which reveal strategy earlier than press releases
- Transcripts or notes from conference talks and earnings calls

## Naming

`YYYY-MM-DD_source_short-description.ext`

`2026-09-28_competitor-name_pricing-page-capture.md`

## Before anyone analyzes this

Capture the date you collected it. Competitor pages change without notice, and a screenshot with no date is worthless six months later.
