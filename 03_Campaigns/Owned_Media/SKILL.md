# 03_Campaigns/Owned_Media

## What this folder is for

The channels where nobody can take the audience away from us. Email list, blog, newsletter, podcast, community.

## What goes in the data folder

Email platform exports (sends, opens, clicks, unsubscribes by campaign), list growth by source, content performance by post, podcast downloads.

## How work gets done

1. Start with the job the piece does for the reader. If you cannot name what changes for them, do not write it.
2. Check the claim against `02_Customers` and the evidence register. Unsupported claims get cut or marked.
3. Name the one behavior the piece is asking for.
4. Write it, then check length, format, and call to action against the surface it will appear on.
5. Record what was published, when, and what happened.

## The rule that matters most here

Earn attention before asking for anything. The sequence is useful insight, then trust, then a small commitment, then more value, then a paid commitment. Skipping steps costs more than it saves.

## Guardrails

- Consent governs email. Someone who gave you an address for one thing has not agreed to everything.
- Do not publish under a person's name without their explicit approval.
- Volume without a distribution and learning plan is just cost.

## Definition of done

Published item, the behavior it asked for, the number it moved, and the date.
