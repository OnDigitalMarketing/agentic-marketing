# 02_SEO

Read `../SKILL.md` first.

## What this folder is for

Earning position in conventional search results. Slow to move, durable once moved, and measurable in a way most channels are not, because the query tells you the intent.

## Data folders

`00_keyword_data`, `01_search_console_data`, `02_competitor_data`, `03_technical_data`.

## How work gets done

1. Pull what you already rank for and who actually arrives. Separate the queries that match your buyer from the ones that do not.
2. State the gap in clicks from qualified queries, not in average position. Position is a proxy, and it moves without the business moving.
3. Check the technical floor first. Pages that cannot be crawled or that load slowly will not rank however good the writing is.
4. Prioritize by buyer intent first and volume second.
5. Write the recommendation with an expected effect and a date to check it.

## The trap in this folder

Traffic that converts nobody is a cost. A page can rank first, bring thousands of visitors, and contribute nothing, and every report will still show a win. Carry the query through to what the visitor did next, every time.

## Guardrails

- Tool-generated difficulty and authority scores are estimates from a model. Never present one as a guarantee of visibility.
- Ranking changes take weeks to months. A recommendation checked after ten days has not been tested.
- Do not copy competitor content.

## Definition of done

Current state with a date, the gap in qualified clicks, the technical blockers, a prioritized list, and a check-back date.
