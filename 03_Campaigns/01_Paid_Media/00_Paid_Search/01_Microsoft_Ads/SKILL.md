# 01_Microsoft_Ads

Read `../SKILL.md` first.

## What it is good for

Bing, Yahoo, and the search inside Windows and Edge, plus syndicated partners. Volume is a fraction of Google, and cost per click is usually lower for the same query. The audience skews older, more desktop, and more corporate, which suits many business-to-business offers better than the raw numbers suggest.

## Data folder

`00_Microsoft_Ads_data`. Campaign, ad group, keyword, and ad exports, plus the search terms report and the LinkedIn profile targeting breakdown if you use it.

## What differs here

- **You can layer LinkedIn company, industry, and job function targeting onto search**, which no other search platform offers. For business-to-business this is the reason to be here.
- Importing your Google campaigns works and is the usual starting point. Imported negatives and settings drift out of sync afterward, so re-import on a schedule or manage them separately.
- Syndicated search partner traffic converts worse than owned Bing traffic. Segment it and exclude it if it underperforms.

## Guardrails

- Do not judge this platform on volume. Judge it on cost per acquisition at the volume available.
- An imported campaign inherits Google's structure and not Google's performance. Re-baseline before comparing.

## Definition of done

Cost per acquisition against the ceiling, search partner traffic segmented, volume ceiling stated honestly, and a call.
