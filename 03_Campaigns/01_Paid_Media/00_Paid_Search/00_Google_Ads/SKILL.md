# 00_Google_Ads

Read `../SKILL.md` first.

## What it is good for

Almost any query your buyer types, at almost any volume. The default starting point for paid search, and the platform where the most decisions have been handed to automation.

## Data folder

`00_Google_Ads_data`. Campaign, ad group, keyword, and ad exports. The search terms report above all. Auction insights, impression share, and landing page reports.

## What differs here

- **Performance Max hides placement.** It will spend across search, display, YouTube, and Gmail and report one blended number. Use it knowing that, or use standard campaigns when you need to learn something.
- Broad match plus smart bidding expands into adjacent intent quickly. The search terms report is your only view of where the money actually went.
- Recommendations and auto-apply suggestions optimize Google's objective. Turn off auto-apply.
- Brand campaigns look excellent and are mostly not incremental.

## Guardrails

- Check the search terms report weekly, not monthly.
- Smart bidding needs conversion volume to work. Below roughly thirty conversions a month it is guessing.
- Confirm what you told it to optimize for. A campaign optimizing for form fills will find you cheap form fills.

## Definition of done

Non-brand cost per acquisition against the ceiling, wasted queries added as negatives, campaign type named, and a call.
