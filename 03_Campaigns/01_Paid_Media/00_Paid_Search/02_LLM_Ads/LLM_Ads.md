# 02_LLM_Ads

Read `../Paid_Search.md` first.

## What this folder is for

Paid placement inside AI assistants and answer engines, as those products introduce advertising. Treat this as an experimental line item rather than a channel with known economics.

## Data folder

`00_LLM_Ads_data`. Whatever reporting the platform provides, plus your own logs of where placements appeared and what the surrounding answer said.

## What differs here

- **The formats and the rules are changing quickly.** Anything written here has a short shelf life, so check the current documentation before planning rather than trusting this file.
- Your ad sits next to a generated answer you do not control. The context around the placement is a brand risk that does not exist in search.
- Measurement is immature. Expect less granularity than you get from Google Ads, and expect the definitions to change between quarters.
- The audience is self-selected toward people comfortable delegating research to a model, which may or may not be your buyer.

## How work gets done

1. Read the platform's current ad documentation. Do not plan from what was true last quarter.
2. Bound the spend at an amount you are willing to lose entirely, because you are buying information rather than customers.
3. Set the learning goal explicitly. What will you know afterward that you do not know now?
4. Log where your placements actually appeared, with screenshots and the surrounding answer text.
5. Report honestly, including that the sample is small and the surface is unstable.

## Guardrails

- Keep this separate from your proven channels in every report. Do not let an experimental line item move a blended cost per acquisition that decisions depend on.
- Brand safety review before launch. You cannot preview every answer your ad will sit beside.
- This folder is a hypothesis. Say so when you report it.

## Definition of done

Spend bounded and stated, what you learned, where placements appeared, and a continue or stop call with the reasoning visible.
