# 00_Paid_Search

Read `../Paid_Media.md` first. This file covers only what is different about search.

## What search is good for

Capturing demand that already exists. Somebody typed the query, so intent is given rather than created. That makes search the fastest channel to a clean read on whether your offer converts, and the worst channel for a category nobody searches for yet.

## What differs here

- **Intent is the targeting.** Keyword and match type carry more weight than audience settings.
- **The auction prices your competition, not your value.** A rising cost per click usually means a new bidder, not a worse ad.
- **Brand terms are not incremental.** Somebody searching your name was already coming. Report brand and non-brand separately or you will overstate the channel.
- **Negative keywords are half the work.** Budget leaks through queries you never intended to buy.

## How work gets done

1. Pull the search terms report before touching bids. What people actually typed beats what you thought you bought.
2. Split brand from non-brand in every report.
3. Test one thing: keyword set, landing page, or offer.
4. Measure cost per acquisition against the ceiling from `../Paid_Media.md`, not against last month.

## Guardrails

- Automated bidding optimizes the goal you gave it. Check that the goal is the one you meant.
- Broad match plus automated bidding will spend your budget on adjacent intent. Watch the terms report weekly.
- Do not bid on a competitor's trademark without checking the legal position.

## Definition of done

Non-brand cost per acquisition, the ceiling it was measured against, the wasted-spend queries you added as negatives, and a scale, modify, or kill call.
