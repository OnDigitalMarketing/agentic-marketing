# 02_Facebook

Read `../SKILL.md` first.

## What it is good for

Scale and reach across a wide age range, lead generation forms, and retargeting. The most mature ad platform of the group, which means the best tooling and the most ways to fool yourself with its own reporting.

## Data folder

`00_Facebook_data`. Meta Ads Manager exports by campaign, ad set, and ad, with placement split out from Instagram.

## What differs here

- Audience skews older than Instagram and TikTok, which suits many business buyers.
- Lead forms convert well and produce lower-quality leads than a landing page. Track what happens after the form, not the form fill.
- The conversions application programming interface (API) changes reported numbers. Know whether yours is connected before comparing periods.
- Broad targeting with strong creative now beats narrow interest stacking in most accounts. Test it rather than assuming the old approach.

## Guardrails

- Platform-attributed conversions run ahead of verified revenue. Report both.
- A cheap lead that never becomes a customer is a cost, not a win.

## Definition of done

Placement-split results, post-form quality, cost per acquisition against ceiling, sample size, and a call.
