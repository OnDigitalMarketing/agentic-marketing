# 04_YouTube

Read `../SKILL.md` first.

## What it is good for

Explaining something that needs more than a few seconds, and reaching an audience by what they watch and search rather than who they say they are. The one social surface where a two minute video can outperform a fifteen second one.

## Data folder

`00_YouTube_data`. Google Ads exports by campaign and asset, including view rate and watch time. Keep organic channel analytics separate from paid.

## What differs here

- Bought through **Google Ads**, so it shares targeting signals with search. Custom intent audiences built from search behavior are the strongest lever here.
- Skippable in-stream means the first five seconds are the whole auction for attention.
- View-through conversions inflate the story. Report click-through and view-through separately, always.
- Production cost is real. Factor it into cost per acquisition or the channel will look cheaper than it is.

## Guardrails

- Placement exclusions matter. Check where your ads actually ran and exclude what does not fit the brand.
- Do not judge this channel on a two week test. Consideration cycles here are longer than on feed platforms.

## Definition of done

View rate, click-through and view-through reported separately, production cost included, cost per acquisition against ceiling, and a call.
