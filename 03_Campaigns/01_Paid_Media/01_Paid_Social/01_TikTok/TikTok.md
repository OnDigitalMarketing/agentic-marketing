# 01_TikTok

Read `../Paid_Social.md` first.

## What it is good for

Reaching people who are not searching, cheaply, when your offer can be explained in a few seconds of video. Weak for considered purchases with long cycles unless you are only buying awareness and know it.

## Data folder

`00_TikTok_data`. TikTok Ads Manager exports by campaign, ad group, and creative. Include video view and hold rate metrics, since those diagnose creative before conversions arrive.

## What differs here

- **Creative fatigue is fastest on this platform.** Plan new creative every one to two weeks, not every quarter.
- Ads that look like ads get skipped. Native production style outperforms brand film reliably.
- The first two seconds decide whether anything else matters. Measure hold rate.
- Spark Ads, which boost real organic posts, usually beat purpose-built ads.
- Audience skews younger than most business-to-business buyer profiles. Check `02_Customers/Customers.md` honestly before spending here.

## Guardrails

- Verify the regulatory position in your market before building a dependency on this platform.
- Sound matters, and licensing for commercial use differs from organic use.

## Definition of done

Hold rate, creative refresh cadence, cost per acquisition against ceiling, sample size, and a call.
