# 01_Paid_Social

Read `../SKILL.md` first. This file covers what all paid social shares. Each platform folder covers what is specific to it.

## What paid social is good for

Reaching people who were not looking for you. Nobody on these platforms typed a query, so the creative has to do the work that intent does in search. That makes creative the main variable and the main cost.

## What differs from search

- **Creative is the targeting.** Modern delivery systems find the audience from who responds. A better-targeted bad ad loses to a worse-targeted good one.
- **Creative fatigues.** Performance decays on a schedule, and the fix is new creative rather than new bids.
- **Attribution is softest here.** Platform-reported conversions and your own revenue data will disagree. Report both and say which is which.
- **The feed sets the format.** An ad that does not look native to the surface gets scrolled past.

## How work gets done

1. Pick the platform from where your buyer already spends attention, using `02_Customers/icp.md`. Not from which platform you enjoy.
2. Set the acquisition cost ceiling before you build creative.
3. Test one variable. Audience, creative, or offer.
4. Plan the creative refresh before launch, because you will need it sooner than you think.
5. Compare platform-reported results against your own revenue data and report the gap.

## Choosing among the platforms

The folders here cover Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, and Reddit. Add others when the customer file justifies it. Pinterest, Snapchat, and X are worth folders if your buyer is actually there, and are dead weight if they are not.

## Guardrails

- Budget changes need approval every time.
- Do not run the same creative across every platform and call it a test. That is a distribution decision, not an experiment.
- Small samples produce confident-looking cost per acquisition numbers that mean nothing. State the sample size.

## Definition of done

Platform, one variable tested, cost per acquisition against ceiling, sample size, creative refresh plan, and a scale, modify, or kill call.
