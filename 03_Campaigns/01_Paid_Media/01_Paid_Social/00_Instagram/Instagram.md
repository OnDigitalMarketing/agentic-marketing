# 00_Instagram

Read `../Paid_Social.md` first.

## What it is good for

Visual products, lifestyle context, and creator-style content. Reels carry most of the reach now, and static feed placements increasingly serve retargeting rather than discovery.

## Data folder

`00_Instagram_data`. Meta Ads Manager exports by campaign, ad set, and ad. Keep placement as a column, because Instagram and Facebook results get blended by default and they behave differently.

## What differs here

- Bought through Meta Ads Manager alongside Facebook, so **split placement in every report** or you cannot tell the two apart.
- Vertical video is the format. Repurposed horizontal video underperforms badly enough to invalidate a test.
- The first second decides everything. Test hooks before you test audiences.
- Creator-style production often beats polished brand production, which is uncomfortable and true.

## Guardrails

- Do not report Meta results without saying whether the conversion count is platform-reported or matched to your own revenue data.
- Music and likeness rights do not transfer from organic to paid. Clear them separately.

## Definition of done

Placement-split results, hook tested, cost per acquisition against ceiling, sample size, and a call.
