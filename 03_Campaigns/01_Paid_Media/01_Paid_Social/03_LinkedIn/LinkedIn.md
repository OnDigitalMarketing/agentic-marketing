# 03_LinkedIn

Read `../Paid_Social.md` first.

## What it is good for

Reaching a specific job title at a specific kind of company. No other platform lets you buy seniority and function this directly, which is why it is usually the first paid social test for a business-to-business offer.

## Data folder

`00_LinkedIn_data`. Campaign Manager exports by campaign and creative, plus demographic breakdowns showing which titles and companies actually saw the ad.

## What differs here

- **Cost per thousand impressions is several times the other platforms.** The economics only work when customer value is high, so get the Customer Lifetime Value figure before you build anything.
- Targeting by job title is self-reported and stale. Job function plus seniority is usually more accurate than title.
- Document ads and lead generation forms convert better than sending people off-platform.
- Frequency climbs fast on narrow audiences. An audience of 8,000 will see your ad many times in a week, and the creative wears out on a small base.
- The demographic report tells you who actually got served. Check it, because delivery often drifts toward cheaper, more junior inventory.

## Guardrails

- Do not run here without a Customer Lifetime Value number. The cost floor is high enough that this is the platform most likely to quietly lose money.
- Lead form volume is not pipeline. Follow leads through to a qualified conversation before judging the channel.

## Definition of done

Delivered audience by seniority, frequency, cost per qualified lead, cost per acquisition against ceiling, sample size, and a call.
