# 03_Campaigns/Influencers

## What this folder is for

Distribution borrowed from someone who already has the audience. Creators, partners, guest appearances, co-marketing, press.

## What goes in the data folder

Partner lists with audience size and overlap estimates, agreement terms, tracked links and their performance, mentions and referral traffic by source.

## How work gets done

1. Start from audience overlap with `02_Customers/icp.md`, not from follower count.
2. Name the mutual value. If you cannot say what the partner gets, there is no deal, only an ask.
3. Agree the deliverable, the timing, the tracking, and the disclosure requirements in writing before anything runs.
4. Track with a link you control, because you will not get the partner's analytics.
5. Report referral quality, not just referral volume.

## Guardrails

- Paid partnerships require disclosure. This is a legal requirement in most jurisdictions, not a style preference.
- Never write a testimonial or a quote on someone else's behalf.
- Entering a partnership is an external commitment and needs approval.
- A large audience with the wrong people in it is worth less than a small one with the right people.

## Definition of done

Partner, mutual value, deliverable, tracked link, referral quality, and a renew or stop decision.
