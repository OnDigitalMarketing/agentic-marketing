# 03_budget_data

**Source. Read only. Never committed.**

Git ignores every folder ending in `_data`. Only this README is tracked, so real files stay on your machine.

## What goes here

- Available capital for marketing, by period
- Approved budgets by channel and campaign
- Spend to date and pacing against plan
- Committed but unspent amounts, which are not available even though they look like it
- Agency, tooling, and staff costs, since those are marketing spend too

## Naming

`YYYY-MM-DD_source_short-description.ext`

`2026-09-28_budget_spend-to-date_q3.xlsx`

## Before anyone analyzes this

Distinguish available from committed. A recommendation to spend more, written against a budget line that is already spoken for, is the most common way a marketing plan fails at the finance meeting.
